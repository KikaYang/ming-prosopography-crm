# Licence of the files in data/apis/

`karlmann_tangl.ttl` is an extract from the APIS export of the Österreichisches Biographisches Lexikon (ÖBL), `oebl_export_6_2020.trig`, produced by the APIS project of the Austrian Academy of Sciences (ACDH). It is included as a point of comparison for a European prosopographical record expressed in CIDOC-CRM.
